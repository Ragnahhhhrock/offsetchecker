01

How it works

Banks calculate offset benefits inside systems you can't see. Errors are rare, small — and almost never checked. Offsetcheck is the check.

1

Add your loan details

Upload your statements or enter your loan terms. Read-only, encrypted, never shared. We don't touch your money — only your numbers.

2

We re-calculate everything

Every day of interest, every offset credit, re-computed from your actual rate, balance and offset activity. The same rules the bank uses — applied independently.

3

You get a verdict

Verified, Review, or Overcharge — with the working shown, line by line. If you're owed money, you get the exact figure and the evidence to claim it.
