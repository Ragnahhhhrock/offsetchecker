Check your bank in three steps

Banks make mistakes. Their systems are complex. Your offset account interest calculation is done daily — one error compounds into thousands.

1

Upload statements

Drag and drop your mortgage statements. We accept CSV, Excel, PDF, even photos of paper statements. Our parser extracts every transaction automatically and sorts repayments, interest charges and offset activity.

2

We re-calculate the numbers

Our engine totals your mortgage payments and interest charges, recalculates your daily offset balance, applies the correct interest rate, and works out exactly how much interest you should have saved each day.

3

See the verdict

See the gap between what you were owed and what you got — to the cent. If it's significant, we'll draft a professional letter of demand ready to send to your lender.

WHY WE BUILT OFFSETCHECK

The harm is hidden. The maths is not.

In July 2026, the corporate regulator ASIC reported that Australian banks had paid more than $55 million in compensation for mortgage offset failures — accounts never opened, never linked, or quietly linked to the wrong loan. Most customers never noticed: the repayment stays the same while the interest silently rises.

Offsetcheck exists so you don't have to take the bank's word for it. Upload your statements and check the numbers yourself.
