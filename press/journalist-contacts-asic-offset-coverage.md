# Offsetcheck press list — journalists who covered the ASIC offset account story

Prepared 22 August 2026 · Offsetcheck media outreach

## Background

On 28–29 July 2026 ASIC published media release 26-173MR and Report 837, *Offsets, out of mind: Banks fall short on mortgage offset account promises*. The review of eight banks (AMP, ANZ, CBA, Credit Union Australia/Great Southern Bank, HSBC, ING, Macquarie, Westpac) found banks had paid more than $55 million in compensation over two years for offset account failures — offsets never opened, never linked, linked late, or de-linked after loan changes. 86% of reported failures were manual errors; banks had failed to detect 77% of reported failures before ASIC asked.

The list below covers the journalists and commentators who reported or analysed the story, with the publicly published contact channel for each. Email addresses are included only where the journalist or their employer publishes them; otherwise the outlet's author page, tips inbox or contact form is listed.
