The two pieces that started this

ASIC · MEDIA RELEASE 29 JUL 2026

Hidden mortgage offset failures costing Australians millions in lost interest savings

The regulator's announcement of review 26-173MR: banks paid over $55 million in compensation for offset failures, and weaknesses were found in how all eight reviewed banks set up, monitored and managed offset accounts.

"When offset accounts don't operate correctly, the harm can be hidden. Loan repayments stay the same, while customers unknowingly pay more interest and take longer to repay their loan."— Sarah Court, ASIC Chair
Read the media release
ABC NEWS 29 JUL 2026

Mortgage borrowers miss out on millions in offset savings due to bank failures, ASIC finds

The human side of the report. Brisbane homeowner Brooke discovered — only while refinancing — that her offset had been linked to the wrong loan, then fallen off entirely. The bank offered more than $5,000 in compensation, but she had no way to verify the figure.

"We had no idea how much money we were actually out of pocket."— Brooke, offset account holder
Read the article

Brooke's story is the precise problem Offsetcheck solves: never accept a compensation figure you cannot check. Upload your statements, re-calculate the daily offset benefit yourself, and compare it against what the bank actually gave you.

Earlier remediation tells the same story: Bankwest refunded about 10,800 customers $4.9 million in 2017, NAB refunded $1.7 million to 966 customers the same year, and in 2022 the Federal Court ordered ANZ to pay a $25 million penalty over unattached offset benefits. This is not a new problem — it is a newly measured one.
